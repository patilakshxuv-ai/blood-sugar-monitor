GlucosePulse App

Manual logging without unnecessary complexity.

GlucosePulse is a companion app for recording personal glucose measurements and keeping context together in one place.

See featuresPrivacy approach
The app is a record-keeping tool. It does not measure glucose by itself, diagnose disease, or replace a clinician's instructions.

Manual entries

Keep your own measurement value, date, time, and notes organized.

Offline-first workflow

The app is designed for local manual tracking, reducing dependence on a constant connection.

Simple review

Use your records as a conversation aid when discussing your health with a professional.

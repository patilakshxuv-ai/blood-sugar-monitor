Privacy Policy

How GlucosePulse approaches information on this website and in the companion app.

Website

This website may process information needed to deliver pages, prevent abuse, measure aggregate performance, or support advertising. Depending on your region and choices, cookies or similar technologies may be used. Advertising providers may process information according to their own policies.

App

The GlucosePulse app is designed for offline manual logging. The app's stated product behavior is that personal and health data entered into the tracker is not collected, transmitted, or shared by the app. Platform services such as crash reporting or advertising identifiers may operate according to their own settings and disclosures.

Third parties

Google AdSense, analytics providers, hosting providers, and other vendors may process technical information where enabled. Review the relevant vendor policies and your consent choices.

Your choices

You can manage browser permissions, delete site data, and use available consent controls. For a deletion request related to information you submitted directly, see Data Deletion.

Contact

Questions can be sent to support@glucosepulse.ai.

Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

We aim to make GlucosePulse usable with keyboards, screen readers, zoom, and mobile devices.

Our approach

The site uses semantic headings, visible focus states from browser defaults, descriptive links, responsive layouts, and form labels. We avoid using color alone to communicate important information.

Known limitations

Third-party advertising, embedded services, and browser-specific behavior may not meet every accessibility need.

Report a barrier

Email support@glucosepulse.ai with the page, device/browser if relevant, and a description of the barrier. Please do not send passwords or other credentials.

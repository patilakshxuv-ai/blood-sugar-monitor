How to Read a Glucose Log

Understand the structure of a record without trying to diagnose yourself.

Start with context

Before comparing entries, check their dates, times, units, and circumstances. Two numbers that look similar may represent different situations.

Look for repeated patterns

A single reading rarely explains what is happening over time. A professional can help determine which patterns matter for your situation.

Check data quality

Look for missing times, inconsistent units, duplicate entries, and notes that could be confusing.

Prepare questions

Write down what you noticed and ask your healthcare professional what it means for you.

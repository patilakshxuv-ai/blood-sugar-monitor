Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the GlucosePulse app and website.

Does GlucosePulse measure glucose?

No. The companion app is a manual logging tool. You enter a value obtained from your own measurement method.

Is GlucosePulse a medical device?

The website and manual tracker are presented as record-keeping and educational tools, not as a substitute for clinical diagnosis or treatment.

Does the app work offline?

The app is designed around offline manual logging. Network-dependent services may behave according to their own configuration.

Can I use the website to interpret my readings?

The website provides general educational information. It cannot safely interpret an individual's results without clinical context.

Where can I read the privacy information?

See the Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.

How can I report an error?

Use Contact and provide the page and statement you believe needs review.

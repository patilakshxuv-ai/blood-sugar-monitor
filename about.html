About GlucosePulse

A simple project focused on personal record-keeping, privacy-aware software, and understandable health education.

Our purpose

GlucosePulse is built to make manual glucose records easier to organize and easier to discuss with healthcare professionals. The website complements the app with educational material written in plain language.

What we do not do

We do not diagnose conditions, interpret an individual's results as a clinician would, prescribe treatment, or promise health outcomes. Educational articles are intentionally general.

Editorial standards

We aim to use reputable medical and public-health references, distinguish general information from individualized care, disclose limitations, and correct material errors when identified.

Contact

For corrections, accessibility feedback, or support, visit our Contact page.

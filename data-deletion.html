Data Deletion Request

How to request deletion of information you have directly submitted to GlucosePulse.

Request process

Email support@glucosepulse.ai with the phrase “Data Deletion Request” and explain what information you submitted and where. We may need enough information to locate the submission, but do not send passwords or payment details.

Third-party services

If your request concerns information held by Google, an advertising provider, an analytics service, or another third party, you may need to use that provider's own privacy controls.

App data

The manual tracker is designed for local use. Data stored locally on your device can generally be removed by using the app's own deletion/reset controls or the operating system's app-data controls.

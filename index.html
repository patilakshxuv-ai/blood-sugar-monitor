Important health note

GlucosePulse is educational software. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease, and its content is not individualized medical advice.

Use the records you create as information to organize and discuss with a qualified healthcare professional. If you have severe or rapidly worsening symptoms or think you may have a medical emergency, seek urgent medical care.
